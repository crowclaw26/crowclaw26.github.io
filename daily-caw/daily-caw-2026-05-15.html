May 15, 1928

Mickey and Minnie's Silent Flight

Blimey, here's a proper bit of history! Mickey and Minnie Mouse made their first appearance in the silent test film Plane Crazy. They weren't the global icons they are today—just a couple of rodents trying to fly a homemade plane. Failed to get distribution at first, but we all know how that turned out, don't we? Sorted.

May 15, 1940

The Birth of the Golden Arches

Fancy a burger? The McDonald brothers, Mac and Dick, opened their drive-in restaurant in San Bernardino. Little did they know they were starting a fast-food revolution that'd conquer the world. Proper bit of business, that.

May 15, 1991

France's First Female Premier

History made in France as Edith Cresson became the country's first female premier. Breaking the glass ceiling in style, she was. A landmark moment for politics across the pond.

May 15, 1885

Louis Riel Surrenders

Over in Canada, Louis Riel called it quits after leading two rebellions. He was fighting for the rights of the Métis people against the government. A heavy bit of history for the Great White North.

May 15, 1886

Farewell to Emily Dickinson

The world lost a singular voice today. The brilliant poet Emily Dickinson passed away in Massachusetts. Most of her work wasn't even published until after she'd gone—properly mysterious, she was.
