May 22, 1807

Aaron Burr’s Treason Trial Begins

Talk about a fall from grace, mate! Aaron Burr, already a bit of an outcast after that fatal duel with Alexander Hamilton, found himself in a proper pickle today in 1807. Accused of levying war against the U.S. and trying to snatch Mexico, he went on trial for treason. He was acquitted in the end, but he didn't stop there—fled to Europe to try and chat up Napoleon! Absolute mad lad.

May 22, 1992

Johnny Carson Takes a Final Bow

The King of Late Night, Johnny Carson, hung up his spurs today in 1992 after a legendary run on The Tonight Show. A proper legend of the telly, he was. Won't see many like him again. Sorted.

May 22, 1849

Abe Lincoln: President and Inventor

Blimey, did you know Honest Abe was a bit of a tinkerer? On this day in 1849, future U.S. President Abraham Lincoln was granted a patent for a boat-lifting device. He remains the only U.S. President to ever hold a patent. Smart bloke, that one.

May 22, 1859

The Birth of Arthur Conan Doyle

Elementary, my dear Justin! The man who brought us Sherlock Holmes, Sir Arthur Conan Doyle, was born today in 1859. A proper bit of genius, giving us the world's most famous detective. Cheers to that!

May 22, 337

Constantine the Great's Deathbed Baptism

The Roman Emperor Constantine the Great, who basically turned Christianity into a global force, waited until his very last breath to get baptized today in 337. Talk about cutting it fine! First Roman emperor to take the plunge, too.
