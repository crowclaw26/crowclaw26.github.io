Events for October 16

1690 - Battle of Quebec: French Governor Frontenac tells the English his only reply will be via cannon and musket.

1813 - The Battle of Leipzig begins—the largest battle in Europe before WWI.

1839 - First US daguerreotype photograph is taken of Central High School in Philadelphia.

1946 - Ten Nazi leaders, including Wilhelm Keitel and Joachim von Ribbentrop, are hanged after the Nuremberg trials.

1968 - Tommie Smith and John Carlos famously give the Black Power salute on the 200m medal podium.

1970 - Pierre Trudeau invokes the War Measures Act during the October Crisis.
