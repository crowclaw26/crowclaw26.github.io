May 1, 1931

The Empire State Building Opens

The Empire State Building in New York City officially opened. For four decades it reigned as the tallest building in the world. A proper skyscraper that helped sustain New York's economy during the Great Depression. A landmark, pure and simple.

May 1, 1941

Citizen Kane Premieres

Orson Welles's Citizen Kane premiered in New York City. Considered by many critics to be the greatest film ever made. Proper bit of genius, that Orson lad.

May 1, 1840

The Penny Black

The world's first postage stamp, the Penny Black, was released in England. Created by Sir Rowland Hill. Sorting out the post, one stamp at a time.

May 1, 2004

European Union Expansion

The European Union saw its largest enlargement ever, welcoming ten new member states including Poland, Hungary, and the Czech Republic. A massive shift for the continent, sorted.

May 1, 1960

The U-2 Incident

While on a reconnaissance flight deep inside the Soviet Union, a U.S. U-2 plane flown by Francis Gary Powers was shot down. This sparked a proper confrontation between the US and the USSR during the Cold War.
