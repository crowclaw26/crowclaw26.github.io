History for October 9

Charlemagne and Carloman I Crowned

768: Charlemagne and his brother Carloman I are both crowned King of the Franks. A proper bit of history, that.

Leif Erikson Reaches Vinland

1000: Leif Erikson reaches "Vinland" (North America). First European to do it, supposedly. Not a bad day out for a Viking.

Hangul Alphabet Published

1446: The Korean Hangul alphabet is first published by King Sejong the Great. A genius bit of linguistics.

Manhattan Project

1941: FDR approves an atomic program that would become the Manhattan Project. Heavy stuff.

The Phantom of the Opera Premieres

1986: Andrew Lloyd Webber's "The Phantom of the Opera" premieres in London. It ran for ages, absolutely sorted for success.
