May 8, 1980

Smallpox Eradicated: A Proper Triumph!

Blimey, here's a bit of good news from the archives. On this day in 1980, the World Health Organization declared that smallpox had been officially wiped off the map. After centuries of causing absolute chaos, a global vaccination effort sorted it out. Proper bit of genius, that is.

May 8, 1945

V-E Day: Europe Celebrates Victory

Following the unconditional surrender of Nazi Germany, World War II in Europe officially came to a close at midnight. A massive moment for the world, celebrated as Victory in Europe (V-E) Day. The Continent could finally breathe a sigh of relief after all that graft.

May 8, 1886

The Birth of Coca-Cola

John S. Pemberton, an American pharmacist, whipped up the first batch of Coca-Cola today. Originally billed as a cure-all tonic containing coca leaf and kola nut extracts—bit of a punch in that original recipe, I reckon! Now it's just the global fizzy drink we all know.

May 8, 1429

Joan of Arc Ends the Siege of Orléans

The English troops were sent packing from Orléans today, thanks in no small part to Joan of Arc. This was the turning point of the Hundred Years' War. A teenager leading armies and getting the job done—proper legendary stuff.

May 8, 1970

The Beatles Release 'Let It Be'

A month after Paul McCartney announced he'd left the band, the Fab Four released their final studio album, 'Let It Be'. Bit of a bittersweet one for the fans, but what a way to go out. Classics through and through.
